# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Be constructive, critique ideas rather than people, respect privacy, and assume good intent while accepting correction. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive, discriminatory, threatening, spam, or privacy-violating content and may restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed in the repository profile. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.
